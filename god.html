🌍 Divine Isle

You are a god. A tiny island, a handful of followers, and a great deal of divine power. Decide where everything goes — homes, farms, temples, and a stadium for the people to cheer in. Keep them fed, entertained and devout. Sculpt the land. Smite what displeases you. Make it rain.

  • Click / tap to place · Drag to orbit · Right-drag or WASD to pan · Scroll / pinch to zoom
  • 1–0 pick a building · R rotate · T / G raise / lower land · X smite · F make it rain · Esc cancel
  • On touch: tap once to preview, tap the same spot again to place
