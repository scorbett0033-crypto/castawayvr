🏝 Castaway — Driftborn Sandbox

An island full of drowned dead and nothing stopping you. Spawn Driftborn whenever you like, pick a machete, axe or katana for each hand, and take them apart. Swing hard to open wounds, harder to take limbs off. Grab the pieces and throw them.

A / X opens the sandbox menu (point + trigger to choose). Trigger near a body or limb grabs it, release to throw. Squeeze the grips and swing your arms to move; left stick also moves, right stick snap-turns.

⚠ Contains graphic violence, blood and dismemberment
Best viewed in the Meta Quest Browser

Tab / M toggles this menu · G grabs the body or limb you're looking at · click to throw it
