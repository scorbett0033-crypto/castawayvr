Booting…
GRAB & THROWGrip to grab anything near your hand. Point at far objects and hold grip to pull them in. Release while swinging to throw. Use both hands on heavy things.
MOVELeft stick moves (click to sprint). Right stick turns. Push a stick forward in teleport mode to aim. Right B jumps.
WEAPONS & TOOLSTrigger fires or uses. A / X ejects the magazine. Reach to your hip and grip to take a fresh one, then push it into the grip. Rack the toy pistol's slide with your other hand.
MENUY opens the wrist menu (settings, spawner, challenges, travel). Point with your right hand and pull the trigger to choose.
WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump · Shift sprint
RMB grab / hold · release RMB to drop · Q throw · LMB fire / use / press
R reload · F alt action · Wheel push / pull held item · Tab menu · Esc release mouse
